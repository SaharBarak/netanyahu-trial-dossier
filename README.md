# Netanyahu Corruption Trial — Sourced Case Dossier

A neutral, citation-backed reference on the criminal trial of Benjamin Netanyahu
(Cases 1000, 2000, 4000). This repository aggregates **publicly reported** facts.
It does **not** reproduce courtroom transcripts and it does **not** render a verdict.

> Status snapshot (June 24, 2026): Netanyahu concluded his own testimony after
> **98 hearings**. Cross-examination ended; the trial is in its late stages.
> Judges are not expected to deliver a verdict before 2027.

---

## ⚠️ Read this first — scope and honesty boundary

This dossier was assembled from open journalistic and reference sources. Three
hard constraints shaped it:

1. **No verbatim transcripts exist publicly.** Israel's judiciary does not
   publish stenographic trial records online. The "98 sessions" are the hearings
   at which Netanyahu *testified* — not 98 downloadable transcripts. Every
   "session" entry here is a **summary of press reporting**, explicitly labeled
   as such. Nothing here is presented as a courtroom record.

2. **No fabricated content.** Where a fact could not be sourced, it is marked
   `[unsourced]` or omitted. There are no invented quotes, exhibit numbers, or
   testimony reconstructions.

3. **No personal guilt verdict.** This is an active criminal trial of a living
   person with no judgment yet entered. `docs/05-analysis.md` lays out the
   prosecution case and the defense case side-by-side and flags the contested
   factual questions — so a reader can weigh them — but it does not declare
   Netanyahu guilty or innocent. That is the court's role, and as of this
   writing the court has not ruled.

This is a research aid, not a legal document and not legal advice.

## Method

Sources are mainstream outlets (Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post, Haaretz,
Al Jazeera, Bloomberg, NBC, Reuters) plus the Wikipedia "Trial of Benjamin
Netanyahu" article as an index. Outlets carry editorial perspectives; where
accounts diverge the divergence is noted rather than resolved. Inline citations
use `[S#]` keyed to `SOURCES.md`.
