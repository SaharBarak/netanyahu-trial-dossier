# 06 — Transcript Availability: Research Findings

**Question:** Do verbatim transcripts of the Netanyahu trial hearings exist
publicly, anywhere on the web?

**Finding (2026-06-24): No.** There is no public corpus of verbatim hearing
transcripts. This is a documented structural fact, established below — not a
limitation of search effort.

---

## Why no transcript exists to obtain

### 1. No recordings were ever made public
The Jerusalem District Court ruled the trial would **not** be broadcast live and
rejected media requests; Israeli law prohibits transmitting courtroom video or
audio without special permission, granted in only a handful of cases ever. [R1]
An October 2025 ruling went further — Netanyahu no longer enters the courtroom
past the press, reducing even photography. [R2] **No audio/video master exists
to transcribe.**

### 2. The court's own case system restricts the written record
Routine inspection of the Net-HaMishpat docket for case **67104-01-20**
(*State of Israel v. Netanyahu et al.*) found only **16 decisions** public while
filing numbers ran into the 40s — in a case nominally "Open to the Public." An
inspection request was denied by the three-judge panel (June 14, 2020); Human
Rights Alert (Dr. Joseph Zernik) won a Supreme Court appeal on aspects of
access. [R3] Even so, what is at issue there is **decisions/motions**, not
verbatim hearing protocols — which are not published in the public docket.

### 3. No leak corpus
No leaked or downloadable verbatim hearing protocols (e.g., Filber or Hefetz
cross-examination) were found. Items that surface in searches are different
artifacts: a leaked **police interrogation** recording (pre-trial), and a leaked
**diary** — neither is a trial transcript. [R4]

### 4. ICC documents are a different matter
Searches repeatedly return ICC-01/18 court records. Those belong to the
**International Criminal Court** Gaza arrest-warrant proceedings — unrelated to
this domestic corruption trial. Excluded as out of scope.

---

## What *does* exist publicly (the finite real set)

| Artifact | Nature | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-trial speech, 24 May 2020 | Full text primary doc | A statement, **not** testimony. [R5] |
| Per-hearing live-blogs / reporting | Paraphrase + short quotes | ToI, JPost, Haaretz, etc. Closest thing to a session record. |
| Court decisions (partial) | Rulings, not transcripts | Some on Net-HaMishpat; access litigated. [R3] |

The honest consequence: a request to "find all the transcripts" resolves to an
**empty set**. Any document claiming to be a full verbatim transcript of these
hearings should be treated as suspect until verified against the court file.
