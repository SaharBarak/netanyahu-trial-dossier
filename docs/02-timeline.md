## Three-judge panel

The case is heard by a three-judge panel of the Jerusalem District Court
(no jury — Israel does not use juries). [S1]

## Note on the "pardon" question

There has been recurring public discussion of a possible presidential pardon.
A pardon is a separate executive process from the trial and is not part of the
court record covered here. [S2][S6]
