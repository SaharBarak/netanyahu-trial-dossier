# 01 — Case Map

Netanyahu was indicted on **21 November 2019** in three cases — the first
indictment of a sitting Israeli prime minister. [S1][S9]

Israeli law distinguishes:
- **Bribery** (`shochad`) — max 10 years imprisonment and/or fine. [S2]
- **Fraud and breach of trust** (`mirma ve-hafarat emunim`) — max 3 years. [S2]

---

## Case 1000 — "The Gifts Affair"

- **Charge:** Fraud and breach of trust. [S2]
- **Allegation:** Netanyahu and his wife Sara received luxury goods — reported
  around **US$200,000** worth, including cigars and champagne — over years from
  Hollywood producer **Arnon Milchan** and Australian billionaire **James
  Packer**, allegedly as an ongoing supply tied to Netanyahu acting in Milchan's
  interests (e.g., US visa, tax matters). [S2]
- **Opened:** December 2016. [S2]
- **Defendant:** Netanyahu (Case 1000 is not a bribery count). [S2]

## Case 2000 — "The Yedioth / Israel Hayom Affair"

- **Charge:** Fraud and breach of trust. [S2]
- **Allegation:** Between 2008–2014 Netanyahu negotiated with **Arnon "Noni"
  Mozes**, publisher of daily *Yedioth Ahronoth*, for more favorable coverage in
  exchange for Netanyahu advancing legislation to curb circulation of rival
  free daily *Israel Hayom*. [S2]
- **Note:** Prosecutors did not charge this as bribery; the alleged deal was
  never consummated. Charged as fraud/breach of trust. [S2]

## Case 4000 — "The Bezeq / Walla Affair" (most serious)

- **Charge:** **Bribery**, plus fraud and breach of trust. [S2]
- **Allegation:** Netanyahu granted regulatory decisions favorable to telecom
  giant **Bezeq** — including approving a 2015 Bezeq–Yes satellite merger — worth
  a reported very large sum to controlling shareholder **Shaul Elovitch**. In
  return, Elovitch allegedly let Netanyahu and associates direct favorable
  coverage on the **Walla!** news site, which the Elovitches owned — reportedly
  across roughly 300 interventions. [S2][S5]
- **Co-defendants:** Shaul Elovitch and Iris Elovitch; Arnon Mozes is a
  co-defendant in Case 2000. [S1]
- **Why "most serious":** it carries the only bribery count, with the 10-year
  exposure. [S2]

## Penalty exposure summary

| Case | Top charge | Max term |
|------|-----------|----------|
| 1000 | Fraud / breach of trust | 3 yrs |
| 2000 | Fraud / breach of trust | 3 yrs |
| 4000 | **Bribery** (+ fraud/BoT) | **10 yrs** |

Netanyahu denies all charges and has called them politically motivated. [S6]
