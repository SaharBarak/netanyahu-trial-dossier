# 03 — Session Log & the "98 Hearings"

## What "98" actually refers to

The widely-cited figure is **98 hearings at which Netanyahu personally
testified**, spanning **10 December 2024 → 24 June 2026**: direct examination by
his own counsel, then prosecution cross-examination, then limited
re-examination. [S3][S4] It is **not**:

- 98 sessions of the entire trial (the trial has run since 2020 with far more
  hearings across the prosecution and defense phases), nor
- 98 published transcripts.

## Why there is no per-session transcript here

Israel's courts do not publish verbatim trial transcripts online. The public
record of each hearing is **journalistic** — reporters present in court summarize
testimony. Producing 98 "full transcripts" would require fabricating a record
that does not exist in the public domain. This dossier therefore does **not**
attempt it. What follows is the **phase structure** of the 98 hearings, with
representative publicly-reported themes — each clearly a press summary, not a
transcript.

## Phase structure of the testimony

### Phase A — Direct examination (from 10 Dec 2024)
Netanyahu, questioned by his own defense, framed the charges as "absurd" and
politically driven; he emphasized his workload as PM and disputed the
prosecution's framing of a quid-pro-quo. [S6][S7]

### Phase B — Cross-examination (from Jun 2025, ~1+ year)
Prosecution-led. Reported focal points:
- **Case 4000 (Walla/Bezeq):** prosecutors pressed Netanyahu on his interest in
  media coverage and his relationship with the Elovitches and with adviser Nir
  Hefetz. [S5][S10]
- **Case 1000 (gifts):** final cross-examination days included Netanyahu
  attributing memory gaps in part to a former lawyer's illness. [S11]
- Netanyahu argued *hostile* Walla coverage and telecom reforms damaging to
  Bezeq undercut the bribery theory. [S7]

### Phase C — Re-examination / clarification (into Jun 2026)
Limited defense re-examination to clarify points raised on cross; defense then
continues with other witnesses. [S4]
