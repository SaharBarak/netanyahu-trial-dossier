## Documentary / recorded evidence (as reported)

- **Hefetz recordings** — large volume of recorded conversations from his years
  with the Netanyahus. [S5]
- **Walla coverage record** — the prosecution's ~300-instance manipulation
  narrative rests on the site's editorial history and witness accounts. [S5]
- **Regulatory decisions** — the Bezeq–Yes merger approval (2015) and related
  Communications Ministry actions form the "give" side of the alleged bribe. [S2]

## Evidentiary soft spots (publicly noted)

- The trial judges **privately advised prosecutors the bribery charge would be
  "difficult to prove,"** and reportedly suggested they consider dropping it —
  which the judges later confirmed. [S7][S12]
- **Filber's inconsistency** (independent action vs. acting on orders) and his
  reclassification as hostile cut against the prosecution's Case 4000 spine. [S5]
- A witness claim of **suppression of exculpatory evidence** surfaced in
  reporting on Case 4000 — contested. [S5]

These are why the outcome is genuinely uncertain rather than a foregone
conclusion in either direction.
